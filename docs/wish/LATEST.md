Protect:
- official Apple and Osprey sources for current product guidance;

- like-for-like comparisons across regions/stores when applicable;
- Osprey use-case and series browsing, with dated US prices maintained only in `osprey/series/catalog.json`;
- a lightweight static site rather than a large product system;
